Privacy Policy for SnapTools

Last updated: 6 October 2026
Application: SnapTools (com.global.snaptools)

The short version. SnapTools does all of its work on your phone. Your photos, documents, scans, QR codes and CashBook entries are never uploaded to us or anyone else. The app contains no accounts, no analytics and no servers of our own. The only component that connects to the internet is Google AdMob, which delivers the adverts that keep the app free.

1. Who is responsible

This app is published by Samkeliso Mliba Hlophe . If you have any question about this policy or about your data, contact samkelisoh@gmail.com . We aim to reply within 30 days.

2. Information SnapTools does not collect

We want to be specific, because "we value your privacy" means nothing on its own.

  • Your photos and images. Background removal, photo enhancement and QR reading all run inside the app on your device. Images are never transmitted anywhere.
  • Your scanned documents. PDFs are assembled on your phone and saved to your phone. We never receive them.
  • Your CashBook entries. These are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. We cannot see them, and they are not backed up to us.
  • Your QR content. Text and links you encode or decode stay on the device.
  • Accounts. SnapTools has no sign-up, no login, and no user accounts. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Contacts, calendar, microphone, precise GPS location. The app does not request or use any of these.

3. Information collected by advertising

SnapTools is free and is funded by adverts supplied by Google AdMob. To show adverts, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares certain data with Google. According to Google's own published disclosure, this includes:

DataWhat it isUsed for
IP address Your device's network address, which can be used to estimate your general (not precise) location. Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
User product interactions Events such as app launches, taps and video views. Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Diagnostic information Performance data such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage. Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Device and account identifiers The Android advertising ID, the app set ID, and where applicable other identifiers linked to accounts signed in on the device. Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention

This data is collected by Google, not by us. We never receive it in a form that identifies you; we only see anonymous, aggregated earnings reports in the AdMob dashboard. Google encrypts this data in transit using TLS.

Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the AdMob policies.

Your controls over advertising

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID. On your phone, open Settings → Google → All services → Ads. You can reset the ID or delete it entirely. Deleting it stops personalised advertising.
  • Opt out of ad personalisation. The same settings screen lets you turn off personalised adverts. You will still see adverts, but they will not be tailored to you.

4. Where your data is stored

Data you create in SnapTools — CashBook entries, your preferred converter settings — is stored in the app's private local storage on your device. It is not synced, not backed up by us, and not accessible to other apps.

This means it is not backed up anywhere. If you uninstall SnapTools or clear its app data, your CashBook entries are permanently deleted and we cannot restore them. Use the Export CSV button in CashBook to keep your own backup.

5. Deleting your data

  • CashBook entries: delete individual entries with the ✕ button, or remove everything with Clear all.
  • Everything the app stores: go to Settings → Apps → SnapTools → Storage → Clear data, or simply uninstall the app.
  • Advertising data held by Google: we cannot delete this on your behalf, because we never hold it. Use the Android advertising ID controls described in section 3, and Google's My Activity page.

Because we hold no personal data about you on any server, there is no account for us to delete and no data export for us to provide.

6. Children

SnapTools is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is not enrolled in Google Play's Designed for Families programme.

7. Permissions the app uses

PermissionWhy
INTERNET Required by Google AdMob to fetch adverts. No other part of the app uses the network — every tool works fully offline.
AD_ID Added automatically by the Google Mobile Ads SDK so adverts can be served. See section 3 for how to reset or remove this identifier.

When you choose a photo or take one for the document scanner, SnapTools uses the Android system file picker. The app receives only the single file you choose; it has no standing access to your photo library.

8. Security

Because your content never leaves your device, the main protection is Android's own app sandboxing, which prevents other apps from reading SnapTools' local storage. Data transmitted by the advertising SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of data handled by third parties such as Google.

9. International users

SnapTools is available worldwide. Because we collect no personal data ourselves, we do not transfer any personal data across borders. Google may process advertising data on servers outside your country; this is covered by the Google Privacy Policy linked in section 3.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data — for example by adding a feature that needs a network connection — we will update this page and change the "last updated" date above. Significant changes will also be described in the app's release notes on Google Play.
