🌍 SnapTools

Everyday tools that work offline.

SnapTools is a small Android utility app. Everything runs on your phone — nothing you open, scan or save is uploaded anywhere.

  • Background Remover — cut a subject out of a photo and save it as a transparent PNG
  • Document Scanner — turn photos of pages into a single clean A4 PDF
  • QR Tools — create QR codes and read them from images
  • Photo Enhance — auto-levels, colour, contrast and sharpening
  • Unit Converter — seven categories, works without a connection
  • CashBook — a simple offline money-in / money-out ledger
